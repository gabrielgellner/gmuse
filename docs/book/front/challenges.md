# About this part

Two readings of the domain model that argue with it, kept because they were worth making.

They are not errata and they are not resolved. The model in Part I is a proposal under argument, and these are the
arguments — one of them disagrees with the other in places. Publishing a design without its critique would present a
settled thing where there is a live one.

Read them after Part I rather than instead of it: both assume the model they are about.
